# Operational evidence research

Load for gov research briefs or suspected service-delivery, billing, staffing or grant failures.

## Accept the brief

Expected fields: decision, scope, known_evidence, identity_joins, mechanism, counterhypotheses, decisive_test, search_tasks, authority_questions, constraints, return_contract. Fill noncritical omissions from context and label assumptions; ask only for critical missing facts. Do not invent identities, allegations or access permissions. The parent skill owns filings and contacts.

## Research procedure

1. Re-fetch underlying primary sources; distinguish full record text from a hosted summary. Recover previous requests before recommending duplicates.
2. Trace authorization → scheduling → delivery → documentation/edits → claim/invoice → payment → correction. Record which link each source establishes, actor/system/custodian, identifiers and dates.
3. Resolve identifiers independently. Name/address matches are candidates; historical affiliations do not prove current contracts. Shared addresses, relatives and ethnicity are not misconduct evidence.
4. Search the alleged mechanism and its strongest counterexplanation. Prefer specific rules, audit criteria, technical field definitions, claim edits, remittances and correction records over generic “fraud” queries. Check the rule effective at the event date, not only today's version.
5. For site scope, enumerate CT host leads then robots/sitemaps and official navigation. For APIs, inspect published metadata/specifications or observed client routes; confirm actual schema, coverage and pagination. Do not invent an endpoint or expose nonpublic data. Verify an action URL's meaning and jurisdiction, not merely HTTP 200.
6. Test negative results: identifiers, date span, suppression rules, payer/program coverage, pagination, parser and a positive control. Label blocked/throttled separately from truly empty. Public claims often omit visit time, payroll, patient identity or managed-care detail; do not manufacture those joins.
7. Seek disconfirming evidence: documented off-site operations, authorized edits, voids/refunds, program-specific rates, valid alternative IDs and corrected deficiencies. Home-care office occupancy does not measure delivered care; an employment headcount is not available labor hours. A grant award is not recurring billing revenue.
8. Identify the minimum decisive record, likely custodian and lawful access path. Existing deidentified audit output or blank schemas may be obtainable even where patient-level records are not. Aggregate records can still require privacy protection. Do not assume an agency must create analysis or release protected data.

## Return contract

Return all seven fields, even if empty with an explanation:

- **findings:** Claim, evidence status (observed record fact / reported allegation / inference / hypothesis / contradicted / unknown), exact source and page/record, retrieval date, what it proves and does not prove.
- **source_coverage:** Sources actually read, full versus partial coverage, time/program/population limits, failed access, negative-result controls. Two mirrors are one source. One primary disposition can establish the disposition, not the truth of every underlying allegation.
- **joins:** Actor/identifier/date relationships established, candidates rejected and decisive missing links.
- **authority_routes:** Body, duty, applicable entity/program/date, remedy, missing applicability element, mandatory/discretionary status, verified deadline trigger, exact verified action URL. Unknown fields stay unknown.
- **competing_explanations:** Each hypothesis, supporting evidence, disconfirming evidence and remaining test. Update the initial theory when the evidence changes it.
- **next_test:** One highest-value feasible next step, expected discriminating result, likely custodian and whether it is already authorized. Include neutral draft questions when helpful; do not send them.
- **blocked_dependencies:** External record/access/permission required, dependent tests, and useful independent work still available. No repeated search loops merely to avoid naming a dependency.

## Applied acceptance cases

- Quiet home-care office: contextual observation only; next test concerns off-site shifts, not a fraud conclusion.
- Official survey reports inaccurate task entries: verify payer/task/claim mapping and payment; do not promote the agency's warning into a fraud adjudication.
- Zero NPI rows in a suppressed dataset: report dataset-scope zero, test identifiers and suppression; do not say no Medicaid revenue.
- Same street address as a convicted provider: do not link without documented ownership, control or transactions.
- Manual EVV edit: inspect original value, reason, actor, timestamp, authorization and paid units; valid corrections are a counterhypothesis.
- Agency sitemap missing: follow official navigation/search; never fabricate a filing URL or assert absence from 404.
- Request pending: pursue independent rules/schema/source work; report the true external dependency after those paths are exhausted.
