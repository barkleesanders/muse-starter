# Operational evidence and the deepsearch handoff

Load before investigating service delivery, public payments, staffing, grants, or suspected operational misconduct.

## Start with the missing connection

Map authorization → scheduling → delivery → documentation and edits → submitted claim/invoice → adjudication/payment → correction/recoupment. Adapt the chain to the program. A public award, license, inspection, or office visit usually covers only one link.

For each link record actor, identifier, date, system, custodian and access route. Separate owner, manager, scheduler, caregiver/employer, time approver, billing vendor, payer and regulator. Link entities only with documentary evidence and dates; preserve historical versus current status.

Ask “What would normal operations look like?” Home-care services occur in clients' homes, so an empty office is weak evidence. Headcount is not FTE or available hours; claims are not necessarily visits; grants are not necessarily reimbursement. Suppressed or unmatched data does not mean zero activity. A manual EVV correction is not inherently false. Compare equivalent services, units, periods, populations and data coverage.

## Research brief sent to deepsearch

Use these exact field names; plain text or JSON is acceptable. Sending a brief means applying the research workflow, not necessarily spawning an agent.

- **decision:** What action or conclusion will the research change?
- **scope:** Entity, program, jurisdiction, date range, locations, exclusions.
- **known_evidence:** Source URLs/files, exact record/page, date and evidence status. Earlier AI summaries are leads.
- **identity_joins:** Proven links and unresolved NPI/MPI/location/ownership relationships.
- **mechanism:** Specific suspected failure in the service-to-payment chain.
- **counterhypotheses:** At least one plausible innocent explanation and its predicted evidence.
- **decisive_test:** Minimum records/observations that could support OR refute the concern.
- **search_tasks:** Bounded questions; include official rules in force at the event date, record schemas, actual custodian and disconfirming searches.
- **authority_questions:** Which duty, responsible body, remedy and deadline could apply, and what missing fact controls applicability?
- **constraints:** Authorized access/contact/publication, privacy limits, prior requests, unavailable systems and completed searches.
- **return_contract:** findings, source_coverage, joins, authority_routes, competing_explanations, next_test, blocked_dependencies.

## Consume the response and direct the next round

Accept only claims with precise provenance and appropriate status. Check whether the response answers the decision, rather than accumulating related stories. A directory match is not proof of payment; a nearby prosecuted business is not evidence against the target.

Choose the next round by decision value and feasibility. Example: if a survey found incorrect task checkboxes, ask whether those fields affected the submitted claim, not merely whether the software can generate invoices. If guidance separates tasks from billing units, revise the hypothesis and seek encounter-level transmission/audit records. Ask the payer for existing matching rules and available deidentified audit findings, and the regulator for its referral/correction records. Do not assume the public-records custodian possesses a private vendor's complete database.

## Independent reporting tracks

1. Voluntary firsthand accounts: establish dates, role, firsthand knowledge, records and permission to use the account. Ask what happened, who approved it and what documents exist. Avoid leading witnesses. Protect patient and employee details.
2. Lawful public observations: document time, advertised hours and what was actually observable. Do not trespass, surveil patients, impersonate patients or treat one quiet visit as proof of non-delivery. Account for off-site work and appointments.
3. Documentary tests: pair a specific authorized shift with caregiver time, original EVV plus edits, client confirmation where lawfully available, submitted units/code, remittance and adjustments. Missing links stay unknown.
4. Right of reply: prepare neutral, specific questions and verified recipient; distinguish draft from sent. Actual contact requires user authorization. Preserve answers and corrections fairly.

An operational mismatch warrants inquiry; intent, payment and loss each require evidence. Do not compile unrelated criminal or personal dossiers. Limit personal information to what is necessary for the business question.

## Completion and dependency rules

A pending request blocks only its dependent test. Continue independent source discovery, full-document reading, identifier work and question preparation while useful. Stop repeating low-value searches when the next decisive evidence requires an external response or newly authorized contact. Report exactly what could be learned and what remains unavailable. Track in your issue tracker; use native goals only when explicitly requested and their actual completion/blocked rules.
