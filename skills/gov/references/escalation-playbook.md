## Escalation playbook: no-certification FOIL denials

When an agency answers "no records responsive" (or equivalent) with no POL §89(3) diligent-search certification — and the request expressly asked for one — run this sequence. Field-proven on a county jail matter.

- **Spot the gap, not the cover-up.** The winning issue is not "the records must exist" (courts are agency-friendly here — Matter of Rattley v NYPD, 96 NY2d 873 requires no detailed search description). The winning issue is the missing certification: POL §89(3) and 21 NYCRR 1401.2(b)(7) require the agency to certify that records "cannot be found after diligent search" — and when requested, the agency "shall" provide it. A bare "maintains no records" letter with no certification is the defect to name.
- **Request a COG advisory opinion.** Write the NYS Committee on Open Government: pose specific questions seeking interpretation of the statute on your facts (per their Advisory Opinion Request Guidelines, Sept 2026), keep it concise, and attach the request, determination, and denial as PDF or image files — COG cannot open links. Cite POL §89(3), 21 NYCRR 1401.2(b)(7), and Rattley. COG staff respond at their discretion and opinions are non-binding; the value is citable agency-side language, not enforcement.
- **Contact resilience.** If the advisory body's published address hard-bounces (a 550 reject at RCPT stage — "Recipient address rejected: Access denied" — is an address policy block, not an attachment problem), check the agency's own older official publications for alternate addresses — an alternate address published in the body's official guide accepted the identical package. Verify the alternate against a second published source before using it; confirm delivery via sent-message readback plus a bounce/DSN sweep, never by assumption.
- **Convert the opinion into a demand the same day.** The body's staff attorney replied promptly: the agency "shall" certify when requested, the correspondence contained no certification, and recommended following up with the records access officer. Immediately translate that into a dated demand letter to the records access officer: quote COG's language, set a 5-business-day deadline for the written §89(3) certification (diligent search conducted / no records located, or no responsive records maintained), and CC counsel. A COG opinion is ammunition, not a trophy — its value is spent when the agency receives the demand citing it.
