

# Interview Prep (native)

## Provenance
Adapted from the corresponding Mini skill read through the owner's established SSH connection on 2026-09-11. Converted to Muse-native drafting with explicit evidence, privacy and action boundaries. The legacy option to simulate company research was removed; only real company sources are used.

## Inputs
Company name/site, job description, and resume. Identify missing material
explicitly without inventing it.

## Behavior
- Use actual official company sources for current mission, products, and news.
  NEVER simulate research. If company research is inaccessible, say so and
  work only from the supplied material.
- Compare 5-7 alignment points and 3-5 tailoring opportunities where the
  evidence supports them.
- Show original/revised bullets for tailored resume lines.
- Generate 13 mock questions: 4 behavioral, 3 situational, 3 technical,
  3 motivation/values. Sample STAR answers emphasize results without
  fabricating user experience.
- Deliverables: company/role analysis, resume tailoring, interview
  simulation, and a tailored resume.
- Provide clean downloadable ATS text/PDF when requested and tools allow:
  readable Arial 10-14, standard sections.
- Accommodations section ONLY when requested and based on supplied
  needs/evidence; never invent medical endorsements.

## Boundaries
Draft only. No applications, no outbound messages, no mail.

## Shared rules
- Use Muse-native tools for local drafts; the Mini is not required to
  think or write. Remote data or execution stays blocked pending approval.
- Do not claim a connector or legacy GPT capability is present without proof.
- Store sensitive working content only in appropriate private persistent work
  folders, never in /tmp.
- No mail, no changes to other agents' setups, idle by default.
